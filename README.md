## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Nathan Santos**, a Computer Engineering undergraduate at the **State University of Feira de Santana (UEFS), Brazil**.

My interests lie at the intersection of **software development, computer architecture, and embedded systems**.

I enjoy understanding how computing systems work, from low-level hardware and digital logic to backend services and modern web applications.

My experience includes FPGA development, hardware-software integration, academic projects, and software development.

- 🎓 Computer Engineering undergraduate at UEFS
- 🔬 Undergraduate research in healthcare technology
- 💻 Interested in backend and full stack development
- ⚙️ Exploring embedded systems, FPGA and computer architecture
- 🧠 Interested in algorithms, programming languages and problem-solving
- 🚀 Building projects that connect technology with real-world applications
